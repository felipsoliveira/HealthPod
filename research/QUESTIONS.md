# Core Research Questions

## Primary question

Can a recreational aerosol preserve useful visual/sensory properties while substantially reducing the amount and hazard of material deposited in the respiratory tract relative to conventional thermal vaping?

## Mechanism questions

1. Which aerosol-generation mechanisms provide the highest optical signal per unit emitted mass?
2. Does SAW retain its apparent engineering advantages when temperature, contamination, output and reproducibility are measured together?
3. How do SAW, VMN, Soft-Mist and electrospray differ in size distribution and predicted respiratory deposition?
4. Can plume visibility be partially decoupled from deep-lung deposition?
5. Which device materials contribute measurable contaminants?

## Formulation questions

1. What is the minimum-complexity carrier space supported by inhalation evidence?
2. How do aerosolization mechanisms change carrier chemistry?
3. How do hygroscopic growth and evaporation change particle size between device and respiratory tract?
4. Which ingredients should be excluded before experimental screening?
5. Can sensory properties eventually be introduced without destroying the exposure advantage?

## Falsification

The project should explicitly accept these outcomes:
- SAW is not superior to VMN or another mechanism.
- High optical efficiency does not translate to lower respiratory deposition.
- A minimal carrier still produces unacceptable biological effects.
- No tested aerosol offers a sufficiently favorable profile to justify further development.
