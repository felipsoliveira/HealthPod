# Safety and Research Integrity

HealthPod studies **lower-exposure aerosol concepts**. It does not claim that inhaling an aerosol is healthy, harmless or risk-free.

## Core rules

1. **Bench before humans.** Initial research is physicochemical and preclinical.
2. **Mechanism is not evidence of safety.** Non-combustion, non-thermal or nicotine-free designs still require measurement.
3. **Minimal composition.** Additional ingredients require evidence; novelty, “natural” origin or food safety is not sufficient evidence for inhalation.
4. **No wellness claims.** Vitamins, botanicals or supplements are not presumed beneficial when inhaled.
5. **Measure exposure explicitly.** Distinguish emitted, inhaled and deposited mass.
6. **Publish failure.** Negative results and terminated candidates are scientifically valuable.
7. **No DIY human-testing protocol.** Repository work should prioritize characterization and established laboratory methods rather than instructions for self-experimentation.

## Go/no-go philosophy

A candidate must demonstrate favorable evidence sequentially across aerosol physics, exposure/deposition, chemistry/materials and preclinical biology. A strong result in one domain cannot compensate automatically for an unacceptable result in another.

“Lower emission” must identify the compound, normalization basis and comparator. It must not be translated directly into a percentage claim of reduced disease risk.
